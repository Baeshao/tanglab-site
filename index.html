Virus–host interactions across compartments and scales

From viral gene regulation to metabolic rewiring, we study how viruses reshape the cell.

The Tang Laboratory investigates how medically important viruses alter host gene regulation, organelle biology, metabolism, antiviral defense, and disease pathogenesis.

Explore our research Meet the lab

Scientific focus

Across RNA and DNA virus systems, we ask what host-cell changes determine infection success and disease.

  • ✓CMV / herpesviruses: viral gene regulation, nuclear host defense, and viral engineering.
  • ✓Flaviviruses / HCV: organelle stress, lipid biology, metabolism, and replication.
  • ✓Antiviral defense: pathogen sensing, interferon responses, and intrinsic restriction mechanisms.
  • ✓Viral pathogenesis: disease-focused models, including ZIKV-associated cardiac injury.
