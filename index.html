Virus–host interactions across compartments and scales

From viral gene regulation to metabolic rewiring, we study how viruses reshape the cell.

The Tang Laboratory investigates how medically important viruses alter host gene regulation, organelle biology, metabolism, innate defense, and disease pathogenesis.

Explore our research Meet the lab

Scientific focus

Across RNA and DNA virus systems, we ask what changes inside a host cell determine whether infection succeeds and how disease develops.

  • ✓CMV / herpesviruses: gene regulation and intrinsic defense.
  • ✓ZIKV / DENV: organelle stress, lipid biology, and pathogenesis.
  • ✓Host pathways: interferon signaling and metabolic control.
