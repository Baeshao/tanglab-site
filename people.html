People

Members of the Tang Laboratory.

The lab brings together trainees and collaborators working on molecular virology, host response biology, viral metabolism, and disease-relevant cell models.

Principal Investigator

Qiyi Tang, Ph.D., M.D.

Professor, Department of Microbiology, Howard University College of Medicine. Leads research on virus–host interactions, viral gene regulation, and antiviral/vector development.

Lab Manager

Ruth S. Cruz-Cosme

Supports virology, molecular biology, and cell culture projects across the laboratory, with major contributions to flavivirus-related research.

Assistant Professor and Collaborator

Najealicka Armstrong, Ph.D.

Collaborates with the Tang Lab on viral immunology, host response pathways, and infection-associated disease mechanisms.

Ph.D. Candidate, Microbiology

Imaan Muhammad

Studies host responses and viral immune evasion in mammalian cell systems, with a focus on hepatitis C virus-related mechanisms.

Ph.D. Candidate, Microbiology

Kaci Craft

Investigates molecular mechanisms of viral replication, host-pathogen interactions, and viral protein-associated processes.

Ph.D. Student, Biomedical Sciences

Shaokai Pei

Investigates ZIKV-driven cellular reprogramming using two-dimensional cardiomyocyte cultures and three-dimensional cardiac models.
