Research

Virus–host interactions across molecular, cellular, and organoid systems.

The lab studies how viral infection changes host pathways that control metabolism, signaling, replication, and cellular stress responses.

01

Zika virus biology

Zika virus (ZIKV) projects examine how infection alters lipid metabolism, lipid droplet biology, calcium signaling, and cellular function in two-dimensional cardiomyocyte cultures and three-dimensional cardiac organoid models.

  • Cardiomyocyte infection and host-cell remodeling
  • Lipid droplet depletion and fatty acid-linked pathways
  • Calcium imaging and organoid-level phenotypes
02

Dengue virus and flavivirus comparison

Dengue virus (DENV) studies provide a comparative framework for understanding flavivirus replication, host metabolic adaptation, and cellular responses that may overlap with or differ from ZIKV infection.

03

Hepatitis C virus host-pathogen studies

Hepatitis C virus (HCV) projects focus on viral replication control, host response pathways, immune evasion, and antiviral drug evaluation in mammalian cell systems.

04

Human cytomegalovirus engineering

Human cytomegalovirus (HCMV) work includes bacterial artificial chromosome (BAC)-based viral engineering, viral gene regulation, and vector-oriented applications for mechanistic and translational studies.
